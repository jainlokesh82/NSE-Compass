Enter a symbol to begin

Type an NSE symbol such as RELIANCE, TCS, INFY, HDFCBANK, ICICIBANK or CAMPUS. The portal fetches the price history, calculates every indicator locally, evaluates the financials, reads company news headlines, and builds an entry, stop and target plan sized to the amount and risk you set above.

No rating is shown until you analyse a symbol. If a data source is unavailable, the affected section says so rather than showing a zero.

Live data works out of the box — no setup required. It is relayed through free public CORS services since browsers cannot call market-data hosts directly; every figure shows its exact source and retrieval time on the Data Sources tab. Those free relays can occasionally be slow or rate-limited, so for heavier or more reliable use you can optionally paste your own private relay in Settings (see README). Demo mode is also available to explore the portal with clearly labelled synthetic data.
